# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?

Individuals experiencing sleep difficulties who want to understand their reported symptoms, record their sleeping habits, and access reliable sleep health information.

## What exactly is going wrong?

Sleep screening questionnaires, daily sleep diary records, and general sleep education may be accessed separately, making it difficult for users to organize and review their sleep-related information in one convenient place.

## Why does it matter?

Without a structured way to record and review sleep information, users may find it harder to monitor their sleeping habits over time or organize information to discuss with healthcare professionals.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)

Individuals experiencing sleep difficulties may need a convenient way to understand their reported symptoms, record their sleeping habits, and access reliable sleep health information. When preliminary screening questionnaires, daily sleep diary records, and general sleep education are separate, users may find it difficult to organize and review this information consistently. This project aims to develop a Flutter mobile application that combines PSQI and ISI preliminary screening, daily sleep diary recording, and general sleep tips in one platform. The application will be evaluated based on its functionality and usability rather than clinical diagnostic accuracy or treatment effectiveness.
